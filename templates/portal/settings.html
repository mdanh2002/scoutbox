Recover Stalled Operations

Use only when campaigns or background work remain stuck after normal retries. ScoutBox will stop current in-flight campaign/background task state, clear stale local-AI and search-provider coordination locks, restart each affected campaign pass, and restart an interrupted Hidden Leads scan. Other one-off background actions are released but are not replayed automatically to avoid duplicate side effects. Existing Opportunities, Hidden Leads, Address Book contacts, Applications and campaign definitions are not deleted.
