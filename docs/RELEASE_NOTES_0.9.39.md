# ScoutBox 0.9.39

## AI & Discovery presentation

The **Discovery Method:** label now retains its authored Title Case instead of being transformed to uppercase by the older Discovery label style.

Under **Miscellaneous**, Local AI, Cloud AI, and Chatbot timeout controls keep their current values and validation, but their helper text now shows only **seconds** or **seconds per attempt**. The visible `default ...` text is removed; timeout defaults themselves are unchanged.

## Blacklist toolbar

**Reset to Defaults**, **Delete Selected**, and **+** now sit immediately to the right of the **All scopes** filter. The Rows selector and export action remain on the far right.

No database migration or runtime behavior change is required for 0.9.39.
