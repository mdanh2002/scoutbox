# ScoutBox 0.10.102 release notes

## Focus taxonomy
- Focus labels are generated by the configured Local Ollama model from record content; ScoutBox does not ship a fixed Focus taxonomy.
- New AI labels are normalized to natural, slash-free names and the configured maximum-group target is enforced through gradual reuse/merge of AI-created labels.
- Upgrade migration clears the old taxonomy and queues a one-time Local-AI rebuild for every existing Opportunity, Hidden Lead and Address Book row. This full rebuild ignores the normal comparison-record limit; the database migration itself performs no AI/network call.
- Focus selectors are wider so normal group names do not unnecessarily truncate, and Address Book Focus navigation is repaired.

## List-view behavior
- A spinner beside the page title appears while list filtering, client sorting/paging, async refresh or server navigation is in progress.
- Opportunity, Hidden Lead, Address Book, Blacklist, Campaign/Campaign Template and Applications toolbars/footers are reorganized as requested.
- Address Book/Blacklist/Campaign/Template footers show compact item counts without redundant `page 1 / 1` text; rows-per-page/export controls sit on the left and counts/navigation on the right.
- Hide failed URLs is restored at the bottom center of Opportunities, Hidden Leads and Address Book.
- Opportunity/Hidden Lead HTTP status badges move beside the external-link icon in Summary.
- Deleted-row annotations have breathing room before `Deleted on`.

## Recovery safety and sorting
- Blacklisting existing Opportunities moves them to Recycle Bin with `deleted_at`, instead of making them disappear via suppression only. Associated Application/Outreach rows are recoverable with them.
- Explicit restore no longer immediately re-applies the same blacklist within the restore request.
- Remote sorting ranks fully remote first, then remote/hybrid conditions, then on-site/not-remote, then unknown.
- Company Info sorting continues to prioritize larger companies, then company/domain maturity and research confidence.

## Discovery efficiency and tooltips
- Direct public-page acquisition streams responses and skips PDF, archive, media, Office and other binary bodies before download; HTML/text bodies are capped.
- Post Age keeps Post age and Source but suppresses obvious `JobPosting schema / datePosted` evidence. More informative evidence remains visible.
- Company Info and Remote question-mark states do not open empty tooltips.
