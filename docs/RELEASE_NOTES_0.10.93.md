# ScoutBox 0.10.93

- Hidden Leads now evaluates the organization rather than requiring the discovery page itself to look like an About page. Relevant product pages, engineering blogs, case studies, firmware projects, SDR/emulation work and other niche technical evidence can enter a bounded same-domain company-corroboration stage.
- Hidden Leads query planning now rotates the technical-term window every scan and gives each rotated term a query before spending budget on variants. This prevents the fixed query budget from repeatedly covering only the first few profile terms and restores long-tail discovery such as SDR, emulation, firmware, BSP, reverse engineering, VoIP and legacy systems.
- Publisher/editorial pages can be used as discovery evidence without turning the publisher into the company. ScoutBox follows a small number of relevant external links, verifies the actual organization, and rejects unresolved publishers/personal/community pages.
- Recently rejected/suppressed Opportunities can be reconsidered as company-level Hidden Leads when the vacancy failed for a salvageable reason such as expiry, geography, remote/onsite constraints, equipment shipping or a role-specific requirement. Blacklisted, scam, agency/job-board, invalid-company and fundamentally irrelevant records are never salvaged.
- Salvaged leads preserve the original Opportunity/rejection context in `ai_state` and notes, receive a richer multi-factor Hidden Lead score, and receive a penalty when the rejection appears to be a structural company constraint. A company-owned domain is preferred but retained company research can corroborate a company without inventing a domain.
- Hidden Lead scan results now expose a clearer funnel including technical relevance, corroboration, rejection reasons, active-Opportunity overlap and rejected-Opportunity salvage counts.
- Same-domain Hidden Lead corroboration is capped per scan (12 pagesets in Source-guided mode, 16 in Cloud mode) so the broader discovery logic cannot become an uncontrolled crawler. Existing Opportunity, Hidden Lead, Address Book and company-research evidence is reused before any corroboration fetch.
- Show Deleted state is stored independently per recycle-aware list in the authenticated session and persists through navigation/filtering until logout.
- Address Book automatic promotion now derives company summaries from retained Company Info, Hidden Lead summaries, stored company context or company-focused Opportunity evidence rather than relying on a Hidden-Lead-only field. Migration `0116_v01093_addressbook_summary_backfill.py` fills existing blank summaries from retained data without network or cloud calls.
- Forum acquisition keeps the roughly 15-minute cadence but can fail over through up to three sources inside the existing short HTTP budget when earlier sources fail. Repeatedly broken Forum sources receive a temporary cooldown and an occasional recovery probe; AI qualification remains separately bounded.
- Added Hidden Lead provenance display for records salvaged from unsuitable Opportunities.
